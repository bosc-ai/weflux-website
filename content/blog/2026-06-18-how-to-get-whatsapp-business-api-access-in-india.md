<p>To get WhatsApp Business API access in India, create a Meta Business Manager account, complete Business Verification, then set up a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) either directly through Meta's Cloud API or through an authorized Business Solution Provider (BSP). After your phone number, display name, and message templates are approved, you can go live. The full process takes 3&ndash;10 business days, and Meta charges no setup fee&nbsp; you pay only per delivered template message.</p>
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">What is the WhatsApp Business API?</h2>
<p>The <strong><a href="/blog/whatsapp-business-api-vs-whatsapp-business-app" target="_blank" rel="noopener">WhatsApp Business API</a> </strong>&nbsp;officially the WhatsApp Business Platform&nbsp; is Meta's enterprise interface for sending and receiving WhatsApp messages programmatically at scale. Unlike the free WhatsApp Business <em>App</em> (which runs on a single phone), the API has no chat interface of its own. It connects to your CRM, chatbot, or marketing tool and supports automation, multi-agent inboxes, and high message volumes.</p>
<p>For Indian businesses this matters because WhatsApp reaches over 535 million monthly active users in India&nbsp; the single largest WhatsApp market in the world&nbsp; with message open rates near 98%, far above email or SMS.</p>
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">What you need before you start</h2>
<p>Have these ready to avoid delays during verification:</p>
<ul class="[li_&amp;]:mb-0 [li_&amp;]:mt-1 [li_&amp;]:gap-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ul]:pb-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ol]:pb-1 list-disc flex flex-col gap-1 pl-8 mb-3">
<li>A registered business with a legal name, address, and GST or incorporation documents</li>
<li>An active Meta Business Manager account with accurate business details</li>
<li>A functional business website (Meta uses it to validate authenticity &mdash; this is effectively mandatory in India)</li>
<li>A dedicated phone number that is <em>not</em> currently active on the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app</li>
<li>A business email on your own domain (improves approval odds)</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Step-by-step: getting WhatsApp Business API access</h2>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 1 &mdash; Set up your Meta Business Manager account</h3>
<p>Go to <strong>business.facebook.com</strong> and create a Business Portfolio (if you don't already have one). Enter your legal business name, address, website, and contact details exactly as they appear on your official documents. Inconsistencies here are the number-one cause of verification rejection later.</p>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 2 &mdash; Complete Meta Business Verification</h3>
<p>This is the gate that unlocks everything else. In Business Manager, open <strong>Security Centre &rarr; Business Verification</strong> and submit your business documents (GST certificate, incorporation certificate, or a utility bill matching your registered address). Meta cross-checks your details against public records.</p>
<p>Verification typically takes 2&ndash;4 business days. While you wait, you can already start testing with limited messaging.</p>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 3 &mdash; Choose your route and start onboarding</h3>
<ul class="[li_&amp;]:mb-0 [li_&amp;]:mt-1 [li_&amp;]:gap-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ul]:pb-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ol]:pb-1 list-disc flex flex-col gap-1 pl-8 mb-3">
<li><strong>BSP route (recommended for most):</strong> Sign up with a Meta-authorized BSP and complete Meta's Embedded Signup flow inside their dashboard. This guided flow connects your Business Manager, creates your WhatsApp account, and registers your number in one sequence.</li>
<li><strong>Direct route:</strong> Go to <strong>developers.facebook.com</strong>, create a developer account, and create a new app of type Business.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 4 &mdash; Create your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and add a phone number</h3>
<p>Inside the signup flow (BSP) or the Meta Developer dashboard (direct), create your WABA and attach your dedicated phone number. Meta sends a verification code by SMS or voice call. Remember: this number cannot be used on the consumer WhatsApp app at the same time, so always use a separate line.</p>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 5 &mdash; Get your display name approved</h3>
<p>Your <strong>display name</strong> is what customers see when you message them. It must reflect your real business name and follow Meta's display-name guidelines (no generic terms, no misleading claims). Once your business is verified, display name review is usually same-day.</p>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 6 &mdash; Create and submit message templates</h3>
<p>You cannot message customers out of the blue with free text&nbsp; you must use pre-approved <strong>templates</strong> for business-initiated messages. Create your first templates (for example, an order update or a welcome message), assign each the correct category (Marketing, Utility, or Authentication), and submit them. Template approval typically takes <strong>24&ndash;48 hours</strong>.</p>
<blockquote class="ml-2 border-l-4 border-[hsl(var(--border-300)/0.1)] pl-4 text-text-300">
<p><strong>2026 compliance note:</strong> Meta tightened chatbot rules in 2026. AI bots must perform concrete business tasks&nbsp; answering support queries, booking appointments, giving purchase help. Open-ended "AI friend" style chat is no longer allowed.</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-2 -mb-1 text-base font-bold">Step 7 &mdash; Go live and understand your messaging tier</h3>
<p>Once a template is approved, you can send your first live message. New numbers start in a <strong>messaging limit tier</strong> that caps how many unique customers you can message in a rolling 24 hours:</p>
<ol class="[li_&amp;]:mb-0 [li_&amp;]:mt-1 [li_&amp;]:gap-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ul]:pb-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ol]:pb-1 list-decimal flex flex-col gap-1 pl-8 mb-3">
<li><strong>Tier 1:</strong> 1,000 customers/day</li>
<li><strong>Tier 2:</strong> 10,000 customers/day</li>
<li><strong>Tier 3:</strong> 100,000 customers/day</li>
<li><strong>Tier 4:</strong> Unlimited</li>
</ol>
<p>You move up automatically by sending quality messages and maintaining a high quality rating. Keep your opt-ins clean and your block/report rate low to climb tiers quickly during the first 90 days.</p>
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">WhatsApp Business API pricing in India&nbsp;</h2>
<p>Meta charges no setup or monthly platform fee for the API itself you pay per delivered template message. Since&nbsp;1 July 2025, Meta moved from conversation-based pricing to per-message pricing: each delivered template message is billed individually based on its category.</p>

<p>Two cost-savers worth knowing:</p>
<ul class="[li_&amp;]:mb-0 [li_&amp;]:mt-1 [li_&amp;]:gap-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ul]:pb-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ol]:pb-1 list-disc flex flex-col gap-1 pl-8 mb-3">
<li>Service conversations are free and unlimited since 1 November 2024&nbsp; replying within a customer's 24-hour window costs nothing.</li>
<li>Utility templates sent inside an open service window are also free, and high-volume utility/authentication senders unlock lower per-message rates through volume tiers.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you go through a BSP, expect their platform fee on top of these Meta rates.</p>
<h3 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Why WhatsApp API applications get rejected (and how to avoid it)</h3>
<ul class="[li_&amp;]:mb-0 [li_&amp;]:mt-1 [li_&amp;]:gap-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ul]:pb-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ol]:pb-1 list-disc flex flex-col gap-1 pl-8 mb-3">
<li>Mismatched business details between your documents and Business Manager &rarr; make every field identical.</li>
<li>No website or a thin one-pager &rarr; publish a real site with contact info and an About page.</li>
<li>Reusing a number already on WhatsApp &rarr; delete that account first or use a fresh number.</li>
<li>Display name that doesn't match the business &rarr; use your legal or well-known brand name.</li>
<li>Templates written like spam &rarr; keep them clear, transactional, and category-correct.</li>
</ul>
