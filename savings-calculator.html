How the estimate works

For Weflux we pick the cheapest plan that fits your team, numbers and flows, then add only the add-ons you need: ₹250 a month per extra team member, ₹500 per extra number, ₹100 per extra automation, and ₹1,500 for Weflux CRM if you ask for CRM roles. Chatbot building and plain-English AI flows are included.

For the other providers we use the prices on their own pricing pages, read on 6 September 2026, and the cheapest plan that fits your team size. Where a provider's page did not state something, such as WATI's per-message rate card or AiSensy's base plan, we leave it out of the total and say so rather than guess.

All figures are before GST. Meta's per-message charges are not in the totals, because every provider on the official API passes them on; the markup check above shows whether yours adds anything on top.
