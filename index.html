    Built on the official Cloud API

    No middleman. No shared numbers. No markup.

    Your business connects directly to the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. We don't resell numbers, we don't pool messaging volume, and we don't mark up conversation charges. You see what Meta charges, billed at cost.

    Your own verified number

    The phone number you use is registered to your business and verified directly with Meta. Not pooled, not shared, not rented.

    Your own templates

    Templates are submitted and approved under your business account. Edit, version, and version‑control them inside Weflux.

    Direct, transparent billing

    Conversation charges flow from Meta to you, at cost. Weflux earns from the platform fee only - never the message volume.

    Anti-Spam & Compliance

    Compliance built in.

    Built on the official WhatsApp Business Platform with safeguards that protect both your brand and your customers.

    Consent required

    Weflux only sends to contacts who have given documented, affirmative opt-in. Opt-outs are honoured automatically and cannot be overridden.

    Meta-approved templates

    Every message template is submitted to and approved by Meta under your own business account before it can be sent.

    We don't sell data

    Weflux does not provide, sell, or rent contact lists or phone number databases. Uploading purchased or scraped lists is a breach of our Acceptable Use Policy.

    Quality monitoring

    We track delivery quality and Meta quality ratings at account level and suspend accounts showing patterns of unsolicited messaging.
